Your data & this app

Optional account sync

Local-only mode remains available. When you sign in and sync, all saved planner and diary content—including personal notes—is sent over HTTPS to the Supabase backend configured by the site owner and stored under your account. Focus-task text from the Focus page is not saved or sent. Use Turn off sync or Sign out to stop future uploads; signing out withdraws stored consent but preserves local records. A request already sent may finish. Account sync is not end-to-end encrypted.

Account sync is not configured for this copy of Daymark.

Local data & backups

Planner records are saved in this browser's IndexedDB database. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access them; browser storage is not encrypted by Daymark.

The browser may grant persistent storage, but cannot guarantee years of data if site data is cleared, a device is lost, or storage is removed. Export regular backups and keep copies somewhere private.

Open, free and easy to share

Daymark stays a static website with no ads, trackers, or device-control permissions. You can host it with GitHub Pages or install it as an app when served over HTTPS.

Printing uses your browser's print dialog. Choose A4 paper and “Save as PDF” for a digital copy.

Need a fresh start? Export a backup first, then clear this site's saved data in your browser settings. This app does not silently erase your work.
